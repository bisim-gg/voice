# Code of conduct

We want @bisim/voice to be a friendly place for everyone, whatever their experience, background or language.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient, in English and in Persian.
- Assume good intent, and explain rather than dismiss.
- Give and take constructive feedback about code, not about people.
- Help newcomers find their way.

## What we do not accept

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory remarks of any kind.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or deliberate disruption.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break this code, and may block people
who keep breaking it. To report a problem, write privately to the maintainers at **security@bisim.gg**;
reports are handled in confidence.
